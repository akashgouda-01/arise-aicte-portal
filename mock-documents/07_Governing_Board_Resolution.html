EXTRACT OF THE RESOLUTION PASSED AT THE 48TH GOVERNING BOARD MEETING
Held on 10th January 2026 at the Board Room, Technology Block, CITE Campus, Chennai

RESOLUTION NO: CITE/GB/2026/RES-04

UNANIMOUSLY RESOLVED THAT:

  1. The Governing Board of Chennai Metropolitan Trust for Higher Education hereby authorizes the Principal / Secretary, Chennai Institute of Technology & Engineering (CITE) to submit the online application on the AICTE Central Web Portal for Extension of Approval (EoA) and introducing new intake in B.Tech. Artificial Intelligence & Data Science (120 Seats) for the Academic Year 2026-27.
  2. The Board confirms that the campus land of 12,500 sq. metres in Survey Nos. 104/1A and 104/2B, Anna Salai, Guindy, Chennai is in continuous and peaceful possession under a registered 30-year lease deed without any encumbrances.
  3. The Board resolves to allocate a budgetary fund of ₹ 2,50,00,000/- (Rupees Two Crores Fifty Lakhs) towards establishing 4 specialized GPU AI Computing Labs, expanding the Central Library titles, and recruiting 16 full-time faculty members conforming to AICTE cadre ratios.
  4. Dr. K. Chandrasekaran, Principal and Dr. K. Parthasarathy, Secretary are jointly authorized to execute necessary affidavits, upload statutory evidence, and appear before the AICTE Scrutiny and Expert Visit Committees (EVC).
